Chronology

Physical height is time. Higher = later. Lower = earlier. Press and hold an event, then drag it. Snapped events lock onto chronology levels.

The web

Cosmic threads are the cytoskeleton of the story universe. They can split, merge, and share the same event.

Floating events

Floating events live in the Nulliverse without time. Move them freely, then use Snap Floating when you decide where they belong.

Insertion

Tap a glowing thread, then Insert Between. If no room exists, the later event and everything above it are pushed upward.

Edit Event

Snap Floating Event Into Time

This event will stop being timeless and join the chronology. Later events are pushed upward automatically if space is required.
